## University service banners

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-09-09. Decorative conceptual illustrations, not official campus photographs or mascots. Text labels remain HTML for accessibility. Images are bundled locally:

- `pknu-homepage-banner.png`: coastal campus entrance.
- `pknu-portal-banner.png`: academic services gateway.
- `pknu-whalebe-banner.png`: whale and exploration pathway.

Final prompt set (common prompt followed by each subject):

> Use case: stylized-concept. Generate one wide 3:1 website navigation banner raster image for a Korean university LMS. Cohesive premium airy ocean-blue, white, soft cyan and periwinkle palette; softly lit polished architectural 3D editorial illustration, sophisticated not childish. Left 48% quiet pale blue-white negative space for real HTML title overlay. Main composition in right half, readable in a small 350x120 card. Full bleed, no border, no text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark.

Homepage subject:

> Subject: UNIVERSITY HOMEPAGE. A welcoming modern coastal university entrance and light stone academic buildings, landscaped trees, blue sea horizon and soft cloud sky. Conceptual campus illustration, not a claimed accurate rendering of any specific real building. Inviting institutional identity.

Portal subject:

> Subject: UNIVERSITY PORTAL SYSTEM. Elegant glass-blue digital doorway with floating organized rounded application tiles, a calendar and identity card represented with abstract lines only. A subtle laptop in the foreground and sea-blue flowing background. Communicate a unified academic services gateway, calm and organized, no locks or cybersecurity imagery.

Whalebe subject:

> Subject: WHALEBE extracurricular student opportunities. Graceful small blue whale floating beside an open pathway with a compass, sparkling star, and tiny creative learning objects, a leafy sprig and soft stepping-stone shapes above gentle ocean waves. Symbolic exploration and growth beyond classes. Elegant restrained design, whale is not an official mascot recreation.
